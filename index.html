About

I am a graduate student in Computer Science at Vanderbilt University and a research assistant in the Medical-image Analysis and Statistical Interpretation (MASI) Lab, advised by Dr. Bennett A. Landman.

My work sits at the intersection of medical imaging, machine learning, and research computing. I develop reproducible pipelines that integrate deep-learning segmentation models across local workstations, GPU clusters, and cloud environments. I am especially interested in translating technically robust systems into tools that support clinical research.
