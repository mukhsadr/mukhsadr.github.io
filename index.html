About Me

I am an M.S. Computer Science student and research assistant in Vanderbilt University's MASI Lab, advised by Dr. Bennett A. Landman.

I build reproducible medical-imaging and scientific-visualization tools, with current work spanning robust spleen segmentation, diffusion MRI, and accessible research software. I enjoy turning complex computational methods into practical systems for researchers and clinicians.

Selected Work

FEATURED PROJECT

Robust Spleen Segmentation & Fusion

I developed a reproducible CT framework that compares four pretrained segmentation pipelines and combines their predictions using majority voting, locally weighted fusion, STAPLE, and a learned voxel-wise fuser. The work supports reliable spleen volumetry across diverse public, in-house, and clinical datasets.
